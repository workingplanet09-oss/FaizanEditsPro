Process

Brief, footage, editing, feedback, delivery

Five clear steps from first message to final files. At every stage you know what has happened, what happens next and what I need from you.

Discuss your project
  1. 1

    Brief

    Tell me what you're making.

    Start with a short guided form about your project, audience and goals. The questions adapt to your type of content, so you only answer what matters. I read every request and reply within one business day, then send a fixed-scope quote with the deliverables, turnaround and revision rounds. You accept it, sign the agreement and pay the invoice in your client portal.

    You

    Answer the guided questions (about 3 minutes), then review and accept the quote.

    Me

    I review your answers, suggest a call if it would help, and send a clear quote.

  2. 2

    Footage

    Send everything in one place.

    Upload footage, logos, scripts, references and music straight to your project. Your brand kit is saved, so repeat projects start faster. Once the brief is complete and your files are in, the schedule starts.

    You

    Upload your footage and assets and finish the project brief.

    Me

    I check that everything arrived, set up your project folders and confirm the schedule.

  3. 3

    Editing

    I cut the story.

    I edit for story, pacing, captions and sound, then prepare the first draft. You can follow the status, milestones and expected draft date at any time, and message me inside the project.

    You

    Answer any quick questions I have.

    Me

    I edit, add captions and sound, and prepare the first draft.

  4. 4

    Feedback

    Comment on the exact frame.

    Watch the draft in the review player and click the timeline to leave timestamped feedback. Send your notes as one revision round. Every version is kept, so you can compare V1, V2 and beyond.

    You

    Leave feedback, then send it as a revision, or approve.

    Me

    I reply to each note, mark it resolved and upload the next version.

  5. 5

    Delivery

    Approve and download.

    Approve the version you're happy with. Your final files (the master, social cuts, captions and source files where included) appear on a delivery page, ready to download.

    You

    Approve the final version and download your final files.

    Me

    I prepare the deliverables and keep your project organised for next time.
